You must comply with Discord’s Terms of Service and Community Guidelines. Use of the Bot is only permitted for users aged 13 or older.

3. Intended Use
The Bot provides automated moderation functionality, including:

- Executing moderation commands issued by authorized staff

The Bot must not be used for harassment, targeted punishment, or malicious activity.
