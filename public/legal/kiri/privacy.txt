Kiri Ban Sync — Privacy Policy
Last Updated: July 31, 2026

1. Overview
This Privacy Policy explains what information Kiri Ban Sync (“the Bot”) can access and how it is used. The Bot is designed with privacy first and collects no external data.

2. Data the Bot Can Access
The Bot only accesses data provided by Discord’s API, including:
- User IDs involved in ban actions
- Server IDs
- Moderator command inputs
- Basic guild information required for functionality

This information is not stored, not logged externally, and not shared.

3. Data Storage
The Bot does not store:
- IP addresses
- Emails
- Messages
- Personal identifiers
- Ban history outside Discord
- Any data on external servers

All processing happens in real time and remains inside Discord.

4. How Data Is Used
Data is used only to:
- Execute ban synchronization
- Verify moderator permissions
- Perform requested moderation actions

No data is used for analytics, advertising, profiling, or tracking.

5. Third Parties
The Bot does not share data with any third parties. No external databases, logging systems, or analytics platforms are used.

6. Data Removal
Because the Bot does not store data, there is nothing to delete. Removing the Bot from your server immediately ends all access.

7. Changes to This Policy
Updates may occur to remain compliant with Discord’s policies. Continued use of the Bot means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact
For privacy questions, contact the developer through Discord: Kiri Ban Sync Support.
